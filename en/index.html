WAYS TO BUY

Four ways to buy

Choose the stage at which you buy.

A home we have already renovated

Ready to move in. For each home, we list everything we repaired, such as the kitchen, bath or toilet.

Seller: OHANA HOME. You buy directly from us, so there is no brokerage fee. For the building, we are responsible for anything that does not match the contract for 2 years from handover (the minimum period the law allows when a licensed real estate company is the seller).

Renovation set (リノベセット)

Buy one of our homes before it is renovated, and plan the layout and equipment with us. The house price and the renovation cost are shown separately. Before you decide, we explain in writing who does the work and how it is contracted.

Seller: OHANA HOME. No brokerage fee. The same 2-year responsibility for the building applies.

A home with only the essentials repaired

We repair only what is needed to live there, such as the roof, leaks and the kitchen, bath and toilet, which keeps the price down. We show you everything we did not repair.

Seller: OHANA HOME. No brokerage fee. The same 2-year responsibility for the building applies. Parts we did not repair are handed over in their current condition. We describe that condition in the contract and show it to you at the viewing.

A home you found, through our brokerage, with renovation

Found a house on a Japanese property portal such as SUUMO or athome? Send us the link. We act as your broker and arrange the viewing. For the renovation, you can choose the equipment and colors. Before you decide, we explain in writing who does the work and how it is contracted.

The seller is someone else, so a brokerage fee applies (capped by law). The warranty covers only the renovation work. Some homes cannot be arranged, depending on the conditions of the seller or the seller’s agent.

RENOVATION

Renovation in three levels

A guide for the renovation set and for homes bought through our brokerage. You can also choose individual items.

Level 1

Make it livable

  • Roof and leak inspection and repair
  • Termite and underfloor inspection
  • Water heater replacement
  • Basic care for kitchen, bath and toilet
  • Electrical inspection
  • Full cleaning
Level 2

New kitchen, bath and interior

  • Everything in Level 1
  • Replace the kitchen, bath, toilet and washbasin
  • Re-cover floors, walls and ceilings
  • Adjust or replace doors
Level 3

Layout and exterior

  • Everything in Level 2
  • Change the layout
  • Repaint or re-clad exterior walls and the roof
  • Improve window frames and insulation

Please consult us about renovation. Costs depend on the condition of each home. Before you decide, we give you an estimate for that home and explain in writing who does the work and how it is contracted.

HOW TO BUY

How buying works

One person from our team stays with you from viewing to handover.

  1. Viewing

    We visit the home together. We show you both what we repaired and what we did not.

  2. Application

    Apply to buy. If you plan to use a loan, please tell us at this point.

  3. Renovation planning

    For the renovation set and for homes bought through our brokerage. Decide the layout, equipment and finishes, and receive a formal estimate.

  4. Contract

    Explanation of important matters and the sales contract. If you renovate, a separate contract for the renovation work is also signed. We explain in writing who it is with and what it covers before you sign.

  5. Handover

    We hand over the keys. If anything goes wrong after handover, contact us first.

When you buy through our brokerage, you also sign a brokerage agreement with us, and the sales contract is with the seller.

Costs besides the price

  • Stamp duty on the sales contract
  • Registration costs (registration and license tax, judicial scrivener’s fee)
  • Real estate acquisition tax
  • Prorated fixed asset tax and city planning tax
  • Fire insurance
  • If you use a loan: handling fees, guarantee fees and stamp duty
  • Brokerage fee, only when buying through our brokerage (capped by law)

We give you estimates for each home at the viewing. Tax rules and loan conditions change, so we explain the latest details before the contract.

SELLING

Selling a home or an inherited house

We help sellers too. If you own a home in Japan, these are the ways we can help.

Does this sound familiar?

  • You inherited your parents’ house in Japan and don’t know where to start.
  • You live far from the house, even outside Japan, and can’t look after it.
  • The house is still full of furniture and belongings.
  • The building is old and damaged, and you wonder if it can be sold.

If even one of these applies, please start by telling us about it.

How we can help

Buying vacant homes and land

OHANA HOME becomes the buyer. Old houses, and houses with belongings still inside, can be discussed as they are. No brokerage fee applies, because OHANA HOME is the buyer.

Finding a buyer as your broker

If you would rather take time to find a buyer, we can act as your broker. We lay out the conditions of buying and brokerage side by side and think it through with you. A brokerage fee applies.

Consultation on inheritance and end-of-life planning

Our inheritance counselors (Souzoku Shindan-shi, a private certification) and vacant-home revitalization specialists help sort out your family’s wishes and the procedures that need to be done. For registration and tax decisions, we connect you with judicial scriveners and tax accountants.

Clearing out and sorting belongings

Before anything is disposed of, we look for important items such as photos, letters and bankbooks, and return them to the family. We hold a secondhand dealer license, so items that can still be used are passed on to new users.

Restoring homes for the next residents

Homes we buy are repaired with our partner craftspeople and passed on to the next residents. For kitchens, bathrooms and other equipment, we choose things that can be used comfortably for a long time.

A second opinion on price estimates

If you have an estimate from another company, we can go through the basis of the price and the conditions with you. A written price assessment is also free. It is our opinion of price as a real estate company, not a formal appraisal by a licensed appraiser.

The first consultation, a price estimate from documents, and a site visit are free. For properties far away, such as outside Chiba Prefecture, we will tell you in advance whether any costs apply.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there a brokerage fee?

Not for homes where OHANA HOME is the seller (renovated homes, the renovation set and homes with only the essentials repaired). You buy directly from us, so there is no brokerage fee. If you buy a home you found through our brokerage, a brokerage fee applies. The maximum is set by law, and we tell you the amount for each home in advance.

Can you help with a home I found on a property portal?

Yes, and we recommend it. Send us the link or the details. We check with the seller’s side and help with everything from the viewing to the purchase and the renovation. Some homes cannot be arranged, depending on the conditions of the seller or the seller’s agent.

Can I use a home loan?

Please tell us early if you plan to use a loan. Whether you can borrow from a Japanese lender, and on what terms, depends on the lender and on where you live.

Can parts that were not repaired be repaired later?

Yes. Even for homes with only the essentials repaired, we can give you an estimate for the parts you want repaired after handover.

I know nothing about real estate or inheritance.

Please don’t worry. Very few people are used to inheritance or property transactions. Our vacant-home revitalization specialists and inheritance counselors listen to your wishes, sort out what you want to do and what is possible, and make a proposal.

Can an old house be sold without demolishing it?

In some cases it can be sold with the old house standing. It is important to check the condition of the building, the conditions of the land and the costs, and then compare selling before and after demolition. Whether a tax special provision applies should be confirmed with the tax office or a tax accountant.

Do I need to clear out the belongings before selling?

We can handle the house with belongings left as they are. If there is something important, please let us know in advance. Our rule is that staff look for it and return it to your family.

Which areas do you cover?

Our main focus is all of Chiba Prefecture. For any city or town in Chiba, we accept consultations and price estimates from documents. We also handle properties in the Kanto region, and depending on the property, we accept consultations from all over Japan. Whether we can actually buy or visit is confirmed for each property.

Answers may vary depending on the property and your family’s situation.
